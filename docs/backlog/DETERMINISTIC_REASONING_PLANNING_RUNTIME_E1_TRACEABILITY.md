## Post-E1 classification and frozen baseline

- **OBSERVED** = OBSERVED_E1_FINDING: artifact-recorded event, result, or specification creation within this experiment.
- **SUPPORTED_INFERENCE** = SUPPORTED_ARCHITECTURAL_INFERENCE: a future design requirement motivated by those observations, not an implemented/qualified general runtime.
- **HYPOTHESIS** = UNTESTED_ARCHITECTURAL_HYPOTHESIS: not established by E1; requires separate evaluation and is not promoted to a correctness requirement.

Frozen E1 is SUSPENDED_EXTERNAL_HANDOFF, MIXED_WAIT, E1_RESUME_ALLOWED = NO; 27 roots and 41 slots unresolved; Candidate-3 VALID_UNCONSUMED. All links are read-only evidence. Earlier action counts and statuses in historical reports are not current state. “Observed specification” never means “implemented capability.”
