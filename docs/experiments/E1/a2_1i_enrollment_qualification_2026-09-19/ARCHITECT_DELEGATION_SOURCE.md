ARCHITECT MATERIAL DELEGATION
Future Continuation Enrollment

Effective prospectively from this delegation.

The Architect authorizes establishment of a governed continuation-
enrollment capability within the existing E1 runtime lineage.

This delegation does not modify, reinterpret, replace, replay, or
broaden the accepted bootstrap, C1 authority, R1 identity, or any
historical authority record.

The delegated capability is limited to recording adoption eligibility
for an exact continuation candidate that:

1. identifies the exact current predecessor/runtime lineage;

2. has been independently qualified under the applicable continuation
   qualification policy;

3. is bound by immutable identity/digest to the qualification evidence;

4. is enrolled through an authenticated, append-only authority event;

5. has not obtained enrollment authority merely by creating,
   qualifying, proposing, or presenting itself;

6. cannot use enrollment itself as adoption authority; and

7. remains subject to a separate exact adoption authorization before
   any runtime-head transition.

This delegation authorizes implementation and qualification of the
enrollment mechanism. It does not itself enroll C2, authorize adoption
of C2, establish R2, authorize r13, or authorize any E1 production
effect.

The implementation must fail closed on absent, ambiguous, stale,
replayed, mismatched, or unauthorized enrollment authority.

Historical authority and evidence established before this delegation
remain immutable.

Successful qualification must demonstrate both positive and negative
enrollment cases before the capability may be relied upon for ordinary
continuation.
