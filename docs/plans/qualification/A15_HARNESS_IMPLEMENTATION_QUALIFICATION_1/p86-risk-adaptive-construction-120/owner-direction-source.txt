KGE-Forge — Project-Owner Risk-Adaptive Engineering Direction
Status: OWNER-APPROVED ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTION; NOT YET ISSUED AS EFFECTIVE NORMATIVE AMENDMENT
Owner decision
The project owner agrees that KGE-Forge should use risk-adaptive engineering and qualification. The Planner should make governed decisions under uncertainty, balancing expected benefit, potential loss, reversibility, assurance cost, and resource constraints.
Principles
1. Authority and binding safety/security constraints remain mandatory.
2. Resource budgets are finite authorized allocations, with evidence-informed estimates and reserve margins, not proofs that a task will finish.
3. Failure is an expected engineering outcome; classify it as recoverable, terminal, or uncertain.
4. Bounded retries and revisions may be authorized for reversible activities. They are not inferred for already-issued single-use or zero-retry grants.
5. Qualification effort should be proportionate to risk, reversibility, and the expected value of additional assurance.
6. Do not require future execution-produced results as prerequisites for authorization of the bounded activity that produces them; independently enforceable entry safety controls still apply.
7. Stop on exhaustion or unknown consequential effects; obtain fresh authority before exceeding an allocation.
