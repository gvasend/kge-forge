## Normative qualification authority

Prospective scenarios, normative qualification rules, coverage boundaries and acceptance interpretations require an explicit scoped decision from the project owner or an exact delegate empowered for that mandate under an effective charter.

A decision must bind the precise scenario/rule version and identity, owner/support/requiredness and coverage semantics, disjointness/protection policy, independent qualification evidence, frozen-acceptance traceability and historical/prospective assurance exclusions. Technical design or a passing static check is not normative adoption.

Frozen acceptance obligations cannot be silently changed. A proposed amendment must separately identify and version its exact before/after clauses and assurance effects, preserve the original obligation and historical evidence, and receive explicit project-owner approval. An execution cannot weaken its expected-output oracle or suppress native proof alternatives to obtain a pass. Normative approval alone grants no execution or persistence permission.

## Qualification execution authority

Qualification execution requires a separate explicit bounded authorization identifying:

- Exact qualification subject and permitted operations.
- Exact starting state and input identities.
- Execution environment and qualified executable/invocation.
- Exact output boundaries, protected identities and effects.
- Failure/retry limits and historical exclusions.
- Issuer identity or authorized role, governing charter version, applicable direct/delegated authority and effective/lifecycle evidence.

Activation requires an effective charter, valid issuer power, applicable normative approval, sealed inputs and complete independently determined expectations, protection and failure rules, actual resource/environment permissions and explicit execution/evidence approval. No wildcard native execution, extra variant or implicit retry is permitted. Failure or uncertainty stops qualification. Any allowed retry must have its own exact permission and begin from the approved immutable starting state.

## Qualification evidence authority

Evidence capture, immutable publication, save/persistence, restore, replay and audit require explicit scoped permission. The authorization must identify the artifacts, paths, identity/provenance rules, observations and process boundaries.

Preserve complete native identity domains, source/policy pins, occurrence versus Snapshot/content distinctions, ordered history and canonical bytes. State application is not a persisted occurrence. A persisted handoff cannot prove its writer's exit. Logical calculations do not manufacture native events.

File fsync, immutable hard-link publication, directory durability, writer termination and cold-reader restoration are distinct assurances. Claim only what the selected native contract independently establishes. This charter adds no general crash/power-loss guarantee and transfers no A14 grant. Historical instruments, evidence and native declarations remain immutable.

## Delegation

The project owner may issue narrowly scoped qualification delegations under an effective charter. The [delegation contract](qualification/KGE_FORGE_QUALIFICATION_GOVERNANCE_CHARTER_65/delegation-contract.json) requires exact delegate identity or explicitly designated role, owner-designation evidence, charter content identity/version, scope, permitted mandates/operations, subjects, effective boundary, expiration/revocation rule, resource limits and evidence pins.

Delegation is neither implicit nor transitive. A delegate cannot subdelegate. A title, authenticated participation or technical qualification does not confer power. Normative, execution and evidence authority must each be explicitly included; possessing one does not imply another. Delegate evidence and actual issuing power must be independent of the proposed grant being authorized.

The project owner retains charter/version amendment, supersession and revocation authority. A qualification delegation does not amend this charter or grant general engineering, production, external-system or security-bypass privileges. Unverifiable, expired or revoked delegation fails closed. No delegation is issued by this draft.

## Mandate composition

Under this charter **if actually effective**, one instrument may contain three separately explicit normative, execution and evidence mandates when its issuer is independently empowered for each and each section has an exact subject/version, boundaries, pins and explicit approval. Execution activation remains conditional on prior normative approval and all technical, environment and authority gates. Approval is not evidence that execution occurred, and execution permission cannot change normative scope.

Separate sequential grants are required where issuer power or explicit section/approval boundaries cannot cover all scopes in one instrument. The framework does not universally require three separate documents, and it does not infer combined authority from convenience. The present document merely proposes that composition rule; it cannot be used as effective authority before charter adoption.
